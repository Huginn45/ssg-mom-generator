# SSG Legislative Minutes Style Guardrails

When modifying the minutes generator or writing minutes formats:
1. **Title Block**: Always match: `MINUTES OF THE [NUMBER] LEGISLATIVE MEETING OF THE SUPREME STUDENT GOVERNMENT (SSG) ON [DAY OF WEEK], [DATE]`.
2. **Invocation**: State: `The opening prayer was led by Hon. [Name], [Role].`
3. **Roll Call / Quorum**: Always follow: `The roll was called; [Number] were present, [Number] were excused, and [Number] were absent. A quorum was declared.`
4. **Section V Discussion Header**: The very first line of Section V must detail the approval of the agenda: `"A motion to approve the agenda was raised by Hon. [Name] and seconded by Hon. [Name]. The body approved the motion."`
5. **Agenda & Discussion Labeling**: Section IV (Agenda) uses capital alphabetical lists (`A.`, `B.`, `C.`). Section V (Discussion) headings must match these alphabetical labels (e.g. `### A. [Topic Title]`).
6. **Closing Prayer & Adjournment**: Always insert a Closing Prayer as Section VI (`The closing prayer was led by Hon. [Name], [Role].`) and set Adjournment as Section VII.
